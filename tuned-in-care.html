What is Tuned In Care?

Tuned In Care is a platform that connects families with nannies who specialize in caring for children aged 0-6 years. We believe that quality childcare starts with finding the right caregiver—someone who is not only experienced but also deeply attuned to your child's unique needs.

Our platform ensures quality through a thorough vetting process. All nannies on our platform are required to provide detailed CVs, undergo background checks, and complete a comprehensive course on child development and care for young children. This ensures that families can trust they're connecting with caregivers who have the knowledge and skills necessary to provide exceptional care.

Families can give feedback and ratings, creating transparency and trust within our community. We believe that by combining thorough vetting with continuous education and community feedback, we can provide families with a superior childcare experience.
