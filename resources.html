Resources

Welcome to the Resources page. Here you'll find curated podcasts, videos, articles, books, and websites that can support your journey toward emotional well-being and personal growth.

Sign Up

Dr. Harvey Schwartz | Mind, Body & Soul in Healing Podcast

Dr. Harvey Schwartz hosts "The Mind, Body & Soul in Healing" podcast, featuring conversations with psychoanalysts working in various community settings. The podcast explores how psychoanalytic thinking extends into dialysis units, prenatal clinics, corporate board rooms, community centers, and humanitarian organizations. Episodes include lectures, book discussions, and insights from experienced clinicians.

View

Robert Waldinger: What makes a good life? Lessons from the longest study on happiness

In this powerful TED Talk, psychiatrist Robert Waldinger shares findings from the Harvard Study of Adult Development, one of the longest-running studies on happiness. He reveals what truly makes a good life and offers insights into the factors that contribute to long-term well-being and fulfillment.

View

The Psychodynamic Diagnostic Process | Nancy McWilliams

Dr. Nancy McWilliams, a renowned psychoanalyst and author, discusses the psychodynamic diagnostic process. This video provides valuable insights into understanding patients from a psychodynamic perspective, exploring how unconscious processes, relationships, and past experiences influence current thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

View

What is Clinical Work? | Nancy McWilliams

Dr. Nancy McWilliams explores the fundamental question of what clinical work entails. This video offers deep insights into the nature of therapeutic practice, the therapeutic relationship, and the essential elements that make clinical work effective and meaningful.

View

7 Tips for Parenting Young Children

This video provides practical, evidence-based guidance for parents of young children. Learn seven essential tips that can help you navigate the challenges of parenting and support your child's healthy development and emotional growth.

View

Parenting for Emotional Growth

Based on psychodynamic theories, this comprehensive resource addresses parenting issues pertinent to optimizing children's psychological development and mental well-being. The program focuses on secure attachment, emotional connection, and practical strategies for growth-promoting parenting. When parents understand and can positively handle their child's evolving emotional needs, their rearing strategies better optimize their children's developmental potential.

View

Asperger/Autism Network (AANE)

AANE is dedicated to empowering and connecting the autistic community. The network provides resources, support, and education for individuals on the autism spectrum, their families, and professionals. AANE works to create understanding, acceptance, and inclusion for people with Asperger's and autism.

View

Thoughtful Parenting

Finding clarity in an age of too much information. Thoughtful Parenting provides evidence-based guidance and thoughtful perspectives on parenting challenges. The site offers articles, resources, and expert advice to help parents navigate the complexities of raising children in today's world, focusing on emotional development, relationships, and practical strategies for everyday parenting.

View

MIND in MIND

MIND in MIND creates conversations that bring clinical legacies to life. Through thoughtful, in-depth interviews, MIND in MIND illuminates the work of mental health luminaries, weaving together cutting-edge research with time-honored theories. The platform focuses on pediatric, infant, and adolescent mental health, providing valuable resources for professionals working with children and families.
