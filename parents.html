Support Groups
For
Parents

I facilitate weekly support groups for expecting parents or parents of young children (0-5 years old). These groups meet weekly for 1 hour to discuss challenges.

Parents find support by sharing their experience and learning from each other. As a facilitator, I provide developmentally-informed understanding and suggestions.

The group will ideally meet in person, though the possibility of an online group can be discussed. Exact dates TBD. A minimum of 4 parents is needed for a group to start. Please reach out if you have questions or to pre-register.
